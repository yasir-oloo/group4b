## What to do, in order

1. Everyone connects to the database and runs the three checks on your access sheet. **On their own machine** — not just the group lead.
2. Set up your repository — see `repository-setup-guide.pdf` in the general materials. Two admins minimum.
3. Commit `WORKFLOW.md` to the top of your repository.
4. Fill in your scope note together and commit it as `scope.md`.
5. Week 2: work through the query pack, fill in the data quality form, commit both.

## Two things worth knowing now

Your `trips` table has 28,112 rows. If a query returns a different number, something is wrong — check before building on it.

The data has real problems in it, put there on purpose. There are six kinds. A group that reports "the data looks fine" has not finished week 2.

## What you can share with group4a

Data-quality findings — if you find a broken column, say so. You will both hit
the same potholes and there is nothing to gain from watching them struggle.

Your analysis, your decisions and your dashboard stay yours.
